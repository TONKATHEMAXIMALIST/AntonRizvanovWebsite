Anton Fanisovich Rizvanov

I am a multidisciplinary creator bridging the worlds of acoustic art and hardware engineering. As a Professional Jazz Trumpet Player, Composer, and Music Producer, I craft complex auditory experiences. As an Electronic Engineer, PCB Designer, and Jewelry Designer, I apply that same meticulous attention to detail to the physical world. My work is defined by a singular philosophy: every discipline is a synthesis of art and precision.

Jazz Trumpet

Rooted in improvisation and dynamic expression, my approach to the trumpet balances technical mastery with deep emotional resonance. Whether leading an ensemble or operating as a session player, I bring decades of focused study and live stage experience to every performance.

Music Production

My workflow blends the organic feel of acoustic tracking with advanced digital sequencing. Utilizing tools like Logic Pro on a MacBook, I seamlessly route and process complex MIDI data, sequence multi-layered tracks, and mix audio with the ear of a trained composer and the technical precision of an engineer.

Classical Composition

Drawing on deep foundational theory, I write and arrange complex chamber and orchestral works. My compositions reflect a rigorous understanding of voice leading, harmony, and orchestration, bringing structured beauty to the page before it ever reaches the stage.

Jewelry Design

Transitioning precision from the circuit board to precious metals, I design and construct bespoke jewelry. Specializing in handcrafted custom chains, every link is formed with meticulous attention to structural integrity, weight, and aesthetic brilliance.

PCB Design & Hardware

From initial KiCad and EasyEDA schematic layouts to routing dense circuit boards for manufacturing, I specialize in custom hardware engineering. My passion lies in designing custom electronic musical instruments—including complex multi-layered Tonnetz Grid MIDI controllers featuring overlapping 12x8 FSR matrices and integrated SK6812 LEDs, as well as compact matrixed macro keyboards utilizing ESP32-S2 microcontrollers.

Certified Repair Tech

I am a fully certified repair technician for Apple, Samsung, and Google devices. Utilizing micro-soldering, precision teardowns, and deep diagnostic software, I restore advanced consumer electronics to factory-perfect conditions. Fast turnarounds and uncompromising standards define my workbench.
